Support

Need help with the Team Flo BJJ app? Email phill.w.nunez@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Getting into the app

I signed up but see “Waiting for an invite”

The app is only for gym members. Ask the front desk or an instructor to invite the exact email address you signed up with. Once they do, the app opens on its own.

I see “Verify your email”

Open the email we sent and tap the link, then return to the app. Check your spam folder. On that screen you can tap Resend email to get a new link.

I can’t log in

Make sure you are using the same email address your gym invited. If you forgot your password, email us and we’ll help you reset it.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a post or comment: tap the three dots on it and choose Report. Gym staff review reports within 24 hours.
  • Block a member: tap the three dots on one of their posts or comments and choose Block member. You won’t see their posts or comments.
  • Unblock: Profile > Blocked members.

To report something urgent, email phill.w.nunez@gmail.com.

Deleting your account

  1. Tap your photo in the top right and choose View/Edit Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, posts, comments, journal entries and photos are deleted right away.

If you can’t get into the app, email us from your account’s email address and we’ll delete it for you.

Getting a copy of your data

Email phill.w.nunez@gmail.com with the subject “Data Export Request” from your account’s email address. We send your data within 30 days.
